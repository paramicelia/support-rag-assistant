# Bonus wagering requirements

Wagering (also called rollover or playthrough) is the total amount you must
bet before a bonus converts to real, withdrawable money.

## How it works

If a bonus is advertised as **"100% up to €200, 35× wagering"**:

- You deposit €100, get €100 bonus → balance is €200
- You must bet **35 × €100 = €3,500** in qualifying games before withdrawing
- Bet contributions vary by game (see below)

The wagering counter is shown in **Profile → Active bonuses** as a progress
bar. It updates after every bet.

## Game contribution

Not every bet counts 100% towards wagering. Typical contribution rates at
AurumPlay:

| Game type                   | Contribution |
|-----------------------------|--------------|
| Slots (most)                | 100%         |
| Video slots (specific list) | 50% or 0%    |
| Jackpot slots               | 0%           |
| Live casino                 | 10%          |
| Table games (blackjack, roulette) | 10%     |
| Video poker                 | 0%           |
| Sports betting              | 0%           |

The exact contribution list for each bonus is in that bonus's terms, linked
from the bonus page and from the activation email.

## Max bet during wagering

While an active bonus is being wagered, your **max bet is €5 per spin / €5
per hand**. Betting more voids the bonus winnings.

## Bonus validity

Most bonuses expire in **7 days**. If you don't complete wagering in time,
both the bonus and bonus-derived winnings are removed and your deposit
balance is returned.

## Forfeiting a bonus

You can cancel an active bonus any time from **Profile → Active bonuses →
Forfeit**. Forfeiting:

- Removes the bonus and any bonus-derived winnings
- Keeps your original deposit and any real-money winnings unrelated to the
  bonus

If you want to withdraw but have an active bonus, forfeit it first.
