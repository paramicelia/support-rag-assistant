# Free spins terms

## What are free spins

Free spins let you spin a specific slot at a fixed stake — usually **€0.10 or
€0.20 per spin** — without using your own balance. Winnings are paid as
**bonus money** subject to wagering.

## Typical terms at AurumPlay

- **Eligible game** — free spins are tied to a specific slot (listed in the
  promotion). They cannot be used on any other game.
- **Spin value** — usually €0.10, occasionally €0.20 on higher-tier
  promotions.
- **Time to use** — **24 hours** from crediting. Unused spins expire
  automatically.
- **Wagering on winnings** — typically **35× winnings**. Example: you win
  €8 from 50 spins, wagering = €8 × 35 = €280 in eligible games.
- **Wagering window** — **7 days** once winnings are credited.
- **Max winnings cap** — some free spins cap how much you can keep from the
  promotion (e.g. "max €100 winnings"). Anything above the cap is removed.

## Max bet rule

While wagering free-spin winnings, the **€5 per bet cap** applies the same
way as with deposit-match bonuses (`kb_012`).

## How to claim

Free spins are credited to your account automatically when the qualifying
condition is met (first deposit, loyalty milestone, etc). Open the eligible
game and the spins will be offered on the first reel load.

## Free spins did not trigger

1. Check you are logged in on the same account.
2. Open the eligible game listed in the promo page — not a similar one.
3. If the spins do not appear in the game lobby and more than 10 minutes
   have passed since the trigger event, contact support with your deposit
   ID or reward source.

## Country restrictions

Free-spin promotions are not available in every country — eligibility is
listed on the promo page. If you do not see a promo in your promotions tab
it is not available for you.
