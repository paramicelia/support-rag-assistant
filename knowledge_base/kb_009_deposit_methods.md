# Deposit methods

## Available methods

| Method              | Min   | Max per tx | Confirmation |
|---------------------|-------|------------|--------------|
| Visa / Mastercard   | €10   | €5,000     | Instant      |
| Apple Pay / Google Pay | €10 | €5,000   | Instant      |
| SEPA / Trustly      | €20   | €10,000    | Instant (open banking) |
| Skrill              | €10   | €5,000     | Instant      |
| Neteller            | €10   | €5,000     | Instant      |
| Bitcoin (BTC)       | €20 eq. | 1 BTC    | After 1 block confirmation |
| USDT (TRC-20 / ERC-20) | €20 eq. | 10,000 USDT | After 1 block |
| Bank transfer (manual) | €50 | €25,000  | 1–2 business days |

## No fees

AurumPlay charges **no deposit fees** on any method. Your bank or card issuer
may charge a cross-border fee or a gambling-related cash-advance fee — check
with your provider if you see an unexpected charge.

## Declined deposits

If your deposit is declined, see `kb_010` for the most common reasons.
A declined deposit never leaves your bank account.

## Deposit not showing up

Usually caused by a half-completed 3-D Secure flow or a slow blockchain
network. See `kb_011`.

## Using a card registered to a different name

Not permitted. The cardholder name must match your account profile name.
Third-party payments are rejected on both deposit and withdrawal.

## Cryptocurrency addresses

Every deposit address in our cashier is **unique to your account and
single-use**. Do not reuse an address from a previous session — it will still
credit your account, but it takes longer to match and may show up as a manual
adjustment.

## Supported currencies

EUR, USD, CAD, AUD, NZD, PLN, NOK, SEK. You choose the currency at account
opening and cannot change it later without contacting support.
