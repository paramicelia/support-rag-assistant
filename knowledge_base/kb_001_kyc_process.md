# How to complete KYC verification

AurumPlay is licensed under Curaçao (#8048/JAZ2022-014) and must verify every
player before the first withdrawal. This is a one-time process.

## When you must verify

- Before your **first withdrawal**, regardless of amount.
- If your total deposits exceed **€2,000 lifetime**, even if you have not
  requested a withdrawal yet.
- When requested by the compliance team (random review or trigger event).

You can play and deposit before verifying, but funds remain locked until KYC
is complete.

## Step-by-step

1. Go to **Profile → Verification** in your account.
2. Upload a **government-issued photo ID**: passport, national ID card, or
   driver's licence. Both sides if it's a card.
3. Upload a **selfie holding the same document**. Face and document must be
   clearly visible in one shot.
4. Upload a **proof of address** dated within the last 3 months: utility bill,
   bank statement, or official government letter. Mobile phone bills are
   accepted; screenshots of apps are not.
5. Submit. You will see status **"Under review"** in the same page.

## Processing time

- Standard: **up to 48 hours** on business days.
- Peak periods (weekends, promo campaigns): up to 72 hours.
- Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) cases: up to 7 business days. You will be
  contacted by email if EDD is triggered.

## Common reasons for rejection

- Blurry or cropped documents
- Document expired
- Name mismatch between ID and account profile
- Proof of address older than 3 months
- Selfie without the document in frame

See `kb_004` if your verification was rejected.
