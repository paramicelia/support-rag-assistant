# Why was my deposit declined

A declined deposit means your card or provider rejected the transaction —
the money was **not** taken. Below are the most common reasons.

## 1. Issuer blocks gambling merchants

Many banks (especially in the UK, Ireland, the Nordics and parts of Eastern
Europe) block payments to gambling merchants by default, or require you to
opt in. Solution: contact your bank to allow gambling payments, or use a
different method (e-wallet, crypto).

## 2. Insufficient funds

Your available balance is below the deposit amount or below the amount +
bank fees. Check your bank app.

## 3. 3-D Secure not completed

If the 3-D Secure window closed before you confirmed (SMS, app, or biometric
step), the deposit is cancelled automatically. Retry and complete the
3-D Secure challenge within 90 seconds.

## 4. Card not enabled for online / international payments

Virtual cards, some prepaid cards, and freshly issued cards often require
explicit activation for online / cross-border purchases.

## 5. Fraud filter at the issuer

Large deposits, deposits from a new device, or unusual IP geolocations may
trigger your bank's fraud filter. Usually resolved by responding to your
bank's fraud SMS and retrying.

## 6. Our payment gateway declined

Less common. If our anti-fraud layer flagged the transaction (new device +
new IP + high amount), we decline the first attempt. Retry after a short
pause; a second decline from our side warrants a support ticket.

## What to try, in order

1. Retry with the same method
2. Switch to a different method (Skrill / Neteller / crypto rarely hit
   issuer-side blocks)
3. Check your bank app for an SMS about a blocked payment and approve it
4. Contact your bank to whitelist gambling merchants
5. Contact our support if **all** methods fail — we will check our side
