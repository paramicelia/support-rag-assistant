# Why was my KYC rejected

A rejection is **not** a ban — it means one of the submitted documents did not
meet the verification criteria. You can re-submit right away.

## Most common reasons

1. **Image quality** — blurry, dark, glare covers part of the document, or
   corners cropped out. All four corners must be visible.
2. **Expired document** — the ID or proof of address is past its valid-to
   date (or, for proof of address, older than 3 months).
3. **Name mismatch** — the name on the ID does not match the one on the
   account profile. This is the second-most-common reason after image quality.
4. **Address mismatch** — the proof of address shows a different address than
   the one on the account profile.
5. **Selfie without document** — the selfie must show your face and the same
   ID document in one frame. Two separate photos are rejected.
6. **Edited document** — any sign of digital editing (inconsistent fonts,
   mismatched metadata) results in rejection and a manual review.

## What to do

- Read the rejection email carefully — we include the exact reason.
- Fix the specific issue and re-upload **only** the affected document.
- If the name on your ID genuinely differs from your account (e.g. marriage,
  legal name change), contact support and provide a marriage certificate or
  deed poll — do not change the account name yourself.

## If you re-submit three times and it keeps failing

Escalate to a human agent. There may be a compliance flag we cannot resolve
through the self-service flow. Reach out via **Help → Chat** and mention
"repeated KYC rejection" so the agent can pull your case.

## How many attempts you have

There is no hard limit, but every submission creates a case in the compliance
queue. We appreciate careful submissions.
