# Withdrawal limits

AurumPlay applies per-account limits to protect you and to stay compliant with
AML rules. Limits apply across all payment methods combined.

## Default limits

| Period    | Limit     |
|-----------|-----------|
| Daily     | €10,000   |
| Weekly    | €30,000   |
| Monthly   | €100,000  |

The daily window rolls on a 24-hour basis from the first request, not on the
calendar day.

## VIP limits

VIP tiers (Silver, Gold, Platinum) have elevated limits. Exact values are
shared by your dedicated VIP manager and depend on tier and activity history.

## Per-transaction caps

See `kb_005` for per-method per-transaction caps. A €12,000 payout is valid
against the daily limit but will be split into two SEPA transactions because
the per-transaction cap is €10,000.

## What happens if you exceed the limit

The excess amount is queued — you will see a second withdrawal with status
`scheduled`. It will be released automatically at the start of the next
window (next day for daily, next Monday 00:00 CET for weekly).

## Jackpot wins

Progressive jackpot wins and other win events above €50,000 are paid in
monthly instalments of **up to €100,000** per month by default. The first
instalment lands after standard KYC/SoF verification.

## How to raise your limit

- Play-through and consistent activity over 3+ months usually results in a
  VIP upgrade invitation.
- You can request a limit increase via the VIP desk — not granted
  automatically. A compliance review is performed.

## How to *lower* your limit

For responsible-gambling reasons you can always lower your personal limits
without review — see `kb_019`.
