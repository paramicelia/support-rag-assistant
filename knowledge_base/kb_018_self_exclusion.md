# Self-exclusion

Self-exclusion is a formal tool you can use to take a break from gambling.
Once active, you cannot deposit or wager until the period ends — **not even
with a change of mind**. That's the point.

## How to self-exclude

1. **Profile → Responsible Gambling → Self-exclusion**
2. Choose a period:
   - 7 days
   - 30 days
   - 90 days
   - 6 months
   - 1 year
   - Indefinite (minimum 5 years, then only lifted upon your written request)
3. Confirm. The exclusion activates **immediately** and is irreversible.

## What happens after activation

- You are logged out
- The account is locked from deposits and wagers
- Any open bets settle normally
- Your real-money balance can be withdrawn during the exclusion — see below
- All marketing (email, SMS, push) stops within 24 hours
- Any pending bonus is forfeited

## Withdrawing your balance during self-exclusion

You can still withdraw your real-money balance. Go to Cashier → Withdraw
and request as usual. KYC still applies. The withdrawal is processed
manually to confirm identity and to prevent any deposit-back path.

## Cooling-off vs self-exclusion

For a shorter, softer break, see `kb_020` (cooling-off). Cooling-off is
7–30 days and purely a pause. Self-exclusion is formal and longer and ties
into external registers where applicable (e.g. GamStop in the UK).

## External registers

If you are a UK resident we strongly encourage you to also register with
**GamStop** (gamstop.co.uk) — it excludes you across all UK-licensed
operators. AurumPlay supports GamStop checks at login and registration.

## Getting help

Self-exclusion is a useful tool but it is not therapy. If you need to talk:

- **GamCare** — gamcare.org.uk — free, 24/7 (UK)
- **BeGambleAware** — begambleaware.org — free advice and chat
- **Gambling Therapy** — gamblingtherapy.org — multilingual support

You are not alone, and asking for help is a sign of strength.
