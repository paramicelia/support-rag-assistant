# Withdrawal methods and fees

## Available methods

| Method              | Min      | Max per tx  | Processing time    | Fee  |
|---------------------|----------|-------------|--------------------|------|
| Visa / Mastercard   | €20      | €4,000      | 1–3 business days  | 0%   |
| SEPA bank transfer  | €50      | €10,000     | 1–2 business days  | 0%   |
| Skrill              | €20      | €5,000      | Within 24h         | 0%   |
| Neteller            | €20      | €5,000      | Within 24h         | 0%   |
| Bitcoin (BTC)       | 0.001    | 1 BTC       | 30 min – 4h        | 0%   |
| USDT (TRC-20)       | €20 eq.  | 10,000 USDT | Within 1h          | 0%   |

We do **not** charge withdrawal fees. Your payment provider or bank may
charge their own fees, which are outside our control.

## Key rules

- You can only withdraw to a **method you deposited from** (anti-AML rule).
  If you deposited via card, at least part of the balance returns to that
  card up to the total amount you deposited with it.
- If you mix methods on deposit, we split the withdrawal proportionally or
  contact you to choose.
- Card withdrawals work only where the card issuer supports OCT (Original
  Credit Transactions). If not, we will refund to SEPA instead and notify you.

## Before your first withdrawal

- **KYC must be complete** — see `kb_001`.
- Any active bonus must be either cleared (wagering met) or forfeited —
  see `kb_012`.

## How to request a withdrawal

1. **Cashier → Withdraw**
2. Choose method and amount
3. Confirm
4. Status appears as `pending` then `processing` then `completed`

## Daily / monthly limits

- Daily: **€10,000**
- Weekly: **€30,000**
- Monthly: **€100,000**

VIP accounts have higher limits — contact your VIP manager.
