# Why was my withdrawal rejected

A rejected withdrawal returns the funds to your playable balance and is
always accompanied by an email with the reason. Below are the most common
ones.

## 1. KYC not complete

We cannot process any withdrawal before identity verification. Complete KYC
(`kb_001`) and re-request.

## 2. Active bonus with pending wagering

If you have an active bonus, wagering must be met before withdrawal. The
rejection email will tell you how many € of wagering remain. Alternatively,
you can **forfeit** the bonus to unlock your real-money balance — see
`kb_012`.

## 3. Bonus abuse flag

If our anti-abuse system detected abnormal play patterns linked to a bonus
(e.g. low-risk bets only, coordinated multi-account play, irregular bet
sizing) we may void the bonus winnings and reject the associated withdrawal.
This is reviewed by a human before action. You will be told which bonus
triggered the flag.

## 4. Withdrawal to a method you did not deposit from

See `kb_005`. We can only pay to the original deposit method up to the
deposited amount. If you deposited with crypto and request a card withdrawal,
the system will reject it and ask you to use crypto.

## 5. Failed at the payment provider

Rare: the provider rejected the transaction (closed card, blocked wallet).
We will refund to your AurumPlay balance and ask you to choose another
method.

## 6. Self-exclusion or account suspension

If your account is under self-exclusion or suspension, withdrawals of
real-money balance are still possible but are processed manually. See
`kb_017`, `kb_018`.

## What to do

- Read the rejection email carefully — it names the specific rule
- Fix the underlying issue (complete KYC, forfeit bonus, choose another
  method) and request again
- If the rejection email mentions "bonus abuse" or "compliance hold" and you
  disagree, reply to the email and a human agent will review
