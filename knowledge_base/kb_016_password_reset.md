# Reset your password

## If you remember your current password

1. **Profile → Security → Change password**
2. Enter current password
3. Enter new password (minimum 10 characters, including one number and one
   special character)
4. Confirm

## If you forgot your password

1. On the login page click **Forgot password**
2. Enter your registered email
3. You will receive a reset link within 2 minutes — check spam folder if not
4. The link is valid for **30 minutes** and can be used **once**
5. Click the link, choose a new password that meets the strength rules

If the email does not arrive after 10 minutes:

- Check spam and promotions tabs
- Whitelist `no-reply@aurumplay.example`
- Try requesting again — earlier links are invalidated when a new one is sent

## Password rules

- Minimum 10 characters
- At least one number
- At least one special character
- Cannot match any of your last 5 passwords
- Cannot contain your email local-part or your name

## Reset email goes to an inaccessible address

If you have lost access to the email on file (closed account, changed
provider without forwarding):

1. Contact support with a selfie holding your ID (same as KYC) and mention
   "email recovery"
2. A human agent will verify identity and update the email within 48 hours
3. Once the email is updated, use the standard Forgot-password flow

## After the reset

- All existing sessions are logged out — you'll have to log in again on
  other devices
- If 2FA is enabled, it continues to apply as before
- We send an email confirming the change; if you did not initiate it, use
  the "It wasn't me" link in that email which immediately locks the account
