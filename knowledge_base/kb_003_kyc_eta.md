# Verification processing time

## Standard review

For regular accounts with complete, readable documents:

- **Target: under 48 hours**, business days
- Over 90% of submissions are processed within 24 hours
- You will receive an email when the status changes

Business days are Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 CET. Submissions sent on
weekends are picked up on the next working day.

## Peak period

During major bonus campaigns (e.g. New Year, Euro/World Cup) and on weekends
the queue can grow. In these periods:

- Standard cases: up to **72 hours**
- We will not ask you to re-submit during peak unless the document is
  genuinely unreadable

## Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)

EDD is triggered automatically when any of the following apply:

- Cumulative deposits exceed **€10,000** in a rolling 30-day window
- Account activity pattern flagged by AML rules
- Player is from a jurisdiction under enhanced monitoring
- First withdrawal exceeds €5,000

In EDD the timeline is **up to 7 business days** and you may be asked for
additional documents (source of funds, employment proof). The compliance team
will contact you by email from `compliance@aurumplay.example`.

## How to check status

- **Profile → Verification** — live status
- Status values: `not_started`, `submitted`, `in_review`, `edd_requested`,
  `approved`, `rejected`

## When to contact support

Only contact support about KYC if:

- Your status has been `in_review` for **more than 72 hours** (standard) or
  **more than 7 business days** (EDD)
- You received a rejection you do not understand — see `kb_004`
