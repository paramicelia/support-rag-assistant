# Expired bonus

## What expiry means

Every bonus at AurumPlay has a validity period. If wagering is not completed
within that period, the bonus and any bonus-derived winnings are
automatically removed. Your original deposit and any non-bonus real-money
balance are unaffected.

## Default validity windows

- Welcome bonus: **7 days** from activation
- Deposit reload bonus: **7 days**
- Free spins: **24 hours** to use (the spins themselves); winnings from free
  spins then follow a **7-day** wagering window
- Cashback: credited as real money, no wagering (unless stated otherwise)
- VIP bonuses: per-agreement, check with your VIP manager

The exact window is shown on the bonus page before you activate.

## Why did my bonus disappear

The most common reasons, in order of frequency:

1. **Expired** — wagering window ran out
2. **Forfeited** — you clicked Forfeit (reversible only within 30 minutes)
3. **Max bet rule broken** — a single bet above €5 while wagering
4. **Restricted game played for bonus money** — e.g. playing a 0%-contribution
   game with bonus money
5. **Multiple accounts detected** — bonus abuse protection voids bonuses
   across linked accounts

You will see the exact reason in the bonus history under
**Profile → Bonus history**, with timestamp.

## Can an expired bonus be restored

Generally **no** — expiry is automatic and applies to all players equally.
Exceptions are handled case by case for:

- Technical outage on our side that prevented play during the window
- First-time players who did not receive the activation email
- Medical or family emergencies (rare, human review)

If you believe one of the above applies, contact support with details —
expect a response within 48 hours.

## How to avoid losing a bonus

- Check the validity on the bonus page before activating
- Do not activate a bonus if you will not play that week
- Watch the "wagering remaining" progress bar — we email when less than 20%
  is left with under 24h to go
