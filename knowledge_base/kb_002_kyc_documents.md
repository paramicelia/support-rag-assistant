# Accepted KYC documents

We accept the following documents for identity and address verification.

## Proof of identity (pick one)

- Valid **passport** (photo page, machine-readable zone visible)
- **National ID card** (both sides)
- **Driver's licence** (both sides, plastic card only — no paper permits)

The document must be:

- Non-expired at the moment of upload
- Issued by the country of residence registered on your account
- A full colour scan or photo — black-and-white copies are rejected

## Proof of address (pick one)

- **Utility bill** — electricity, gas, water, internet, landline
- **Bank statement** — must show the issuing bank, your name, address, and date
- **Official letter** — tax authority, social services, municipality
- **Mobile phone bill** — accepted from major carriers

The document must be:

- Dated **within the last 3 months**
- Show your **full name and residential address**, matching the profile
- A PDF or clear photo of the physical document — app screenshots are not
  accepted because they often lack the issuer header

## Not accepted

- Handwritten receipts
- Delivery notes from online shops
- Insurance policies without a clear issue date
- Documents in a language we cannot process (non-Latin scripts without
  translation — contact support first)

## Source of funds (only if requested)

For EDD cases or large deposits we may additionally request one of:

- Recent payslip
- Employment contract
- Proof of sale (property, vehicle)
- Investment account statement
