# My account is suspended

A suspension is a hold on the account while something is investigated. Your
balance is preserved and cannot be lost during a suspension.

## Why accounts get suspended

1. **Failed login attempts** — 10 wrong passwords in 30 minutes → 24h
   auto-lock. Wait or use password reset.
2. **KYC escalation** — compliance found an issue and needs documents. You
   will get an email with the exact request.
3. **AML review** — unusual activity pattern triggered manual review.
4. **Suspected account takeover** — we detected logins from unusual
   locations or a pattern typical of stolen credentials. Account is frozen
   for your safety.
5. **Responsible-gambling review** — a self-exclusion, cool-off or external
   register flag is being applied.
6. **Multi-accounting** — evidence of more than one account per person.
7. **Breach of T&Cs** — e.g. bonus abuse, use of prohibited software,
   providing false information.

## How to tell which one applies

Every suspension triggers an email to your registered address explaining
the category and what you need to do next. Check spam folder too.

## How to resolve

- **KYC / AML** — submit what is requested; review takes up to 7 business
  days under EDD (see `kb_003`)
- **Auto-lock for failed logins** — wait 24h or use password reset
- **Account-takeover suspicion** — contact support; expect to re-verify
  identity and set up 2FA (`kb_015`)
- **Responsible-gambling** — if you requested self-exclusion, the
  suspension is by your design and cannot be lifted during the exclusion
  period (`kb_018`)
- **Multi-accounting / T&C breach** — these lead to permanent closure with
  refund of the deposit balance only. Bonus and bonus-derived winnings are
  forfeit

## Can I still withdraw my balance?

In most categories, yes — real-money balance can be withdrawn to the
original deposit method even during suspension. Exceptions are bonus-abuse
and multi-accounting cases.

## How long will the suspension last

Depends on category. You will always receive a status update within 7
business days of the suspension starting. If you haven't heard back in that
time, reply to the original suspension email.
