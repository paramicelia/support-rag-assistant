# Cooling-off period

Cooling-off is a short, reversible pause from gambling. It is lighter than
self-exclusion (`kb_018`) and is the right tool if you just want a break.

## How long

Choose one of:

- 24 hours
- 48 hours
- 7 days
- 14 days
- 30 days

## How to activate

1. **Profile → Responsible Gambling → Cooling-off**
2. Choose the period
3. Confirm

Activation is **immediate and final for the chosen period** — you cannot
shorten or cancel it early. This is by design.

## What happens during cooling-off

- You are logged out
- Deposits and wagers are blocked
- Any in-play bets settle normally
- Marketing is paused
- Your real-money balance remains in your account and can be withdrawn
  (KYC still applies)

## What happens when cooling-off ends

- Account unlocks automatically
- You receive an email reminder
- No action required from you

## Cooling-off vs self-exclusion vs session limits

| Tool              | Duration         | Reversible | Formal?                 |
|-------------------|------------------|------------|-------------------------|
| Reality check     | 60 min intervals | n/a        | Light nudge             |
| Session limit     | Per session      | n/a        | Personal setting        |
| Cooling-off       | 1–30 days        | No, fixed  | Semi-formal break       |
| Self-exclusion    | 7 days – 5 yr+   | No         | Formal, ties to registers |

## Reality check

An automatic pop-up every 60 minutes of active play summarises your time
and net position for the session. You can change the interval between
30 and 120 minutes, but cannot disable it.

## Repeated use

If you find yourself repeatedly using cooling-off and returning with similar
patterns, consider a longer self-exclusion (`kb_018`) or external support
(GamCare, BeGambleAware, Gambling Therapy). There is no shame in recognising
a pattern and stepping back.
